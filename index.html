Questions

What is The Cosmic Blueprint?

A personalized birth chart reading that runs in your browser. Enter your birth date, time and place to see your Sun, Moon and Rising signs, an interactive chart wheel, and nine chapters on your essence, emotions, aura, love, communication, drive, element balance and cosmic aesthetic.

Do I need my exact birth time?

It helps. Your birth time reveals your Rising sign. Without it you still get your Sun, Moon, Venus, Mercury and Mars readings, and you can add the time later.

How accurate is the chart?

Planet positions and the Rising sign are calculated on your device from precise astronomy models, using your birthplace's historical time zone and daylight saving rules. They are checked against a published reference chart and agree to within a fraction of a degree.

Is it a generic horoscope?

No. Your chart is calculated from your exact birth moment, and each reading is written for that specific placement, such as Venus in Libra or Moon in Scorpio.

Are my birth details private?

Yes. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Your birth details and blueprints stay in your own browser.

Can I make blueprints for friends?

Yes. The full Blueprint saves as many as you like, so you can make one for a best friend, partner or crush.

What do I get with the full Blueprint?

Every chapter, unlimited saved blueprints, a printable PDF keepsake, a phone wallpaper and an Instagram-ready share card, all from a one-time Etsy purchase with no subscription.

What is the difference between the demo and the full Blueprint?

The free demo shows your Big Three, your interactive chart wheel and your Sun chapter in full, with a preview of every other chapter. The full Blueprint unlocks all nine chapters and the downloads.
